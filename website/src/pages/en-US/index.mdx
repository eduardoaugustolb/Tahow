A modular Quickshell shell with an island, command palette, preferences and lock visuals. Run independently or choose the optional Ryoku companions.

## One shared visual system

The island, palette and preferences follow the general light/dark appearance and desktop accent. Component overrides keep individual adjustments separate from general defaults.
